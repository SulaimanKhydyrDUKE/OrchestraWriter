Privacy Policy

OrchestraWriter · last updated 19 October 2026

OrchestraWriter is a tool for musicians to turn audio recordings into orchestral scores. This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address, either typed in when you create an account or provided by Google when you sign in with Google. If you use a password, we store only a salted hash of it.
  • Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture. We request no other Google data and cannot access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.
  • Your recordings. Audio files you upload, the names you give them, their duration, and any scores generated from them.
  • Session data. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and the IP address and browser type associated with it.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, to store and play back your recordings, and to generate scores from them. We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or share your recordings with anyone unless you choose to share a score.

Where it is stored

On Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1). Account data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database and audio files in Amazon S3. Both are private; files are only reachable through short-lived links generated for the signed-in owner.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recording from inside the app, which removes both the record and the audio file. To delete your account and everything attached to it, email the address below and we will do it within 30 days.
